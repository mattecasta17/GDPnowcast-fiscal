# Phase 4.0 gate — release-day cutoff decision (2026-06-04)

**Status:** DECIDED. Resolves gate items **2** (Thu-vs-Fri calendar) and **4**
(headline metric) and **demotes** item **1** (target masking). Item 3 (data panel
truncate-vs-fuller) still open.

**Decision:** the headline backtest scores each quarter's nowcast at an
**as-of `(advance_date − 1 day)` pre-advance cutoff** — the tightest as-of that
provably excludes the BEA advance *and its same-day co-releases*. **Not** a fixed
Thursday calendar; **not** Friday + mask-only-GDP.

Reproducible evidence: `tools/release_day_leak_scan.py` (`scan` / `build` / `run`).

---

## How we got here

Gate item 2 asked whether to move the weekly vintage from Friday to the prior
Thursday (so the final nowcast lands "just before" the BEA advance) — a now-or-never
choice (full manifest regeneration + ALFRED re-fetch). We tested it on a throwaway
branch instead of committing blind.

### 1. Thursday ≈ masking on 2017Q1 — but 2017Q1 is not representative

`run` phase, all three arms on the fresh v2 ALFRED panel, 2017Q1:

| arm | rows | skipped | release-week `y_new` | error vs 0.7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Friday + mask (`US_new`) | 19 | 2 | **2.246138** | −1.5461 |
| Thursday, no mask (`US_new_thu`) | 18 | 3 | **2.246138** | −1.5461 |
| Thursday + mask | 18 | 3 | **2.246138** | −1.5461 |

Identical headline. The `build` diagnostic showed why: as-of Thu 2017-04-27 the only
thing missing vs Fri 2017-04-28 was **GDPC1 itself** → masking the GDP cell on Friday
makes the vintage input-identical to Thursday. Thursday buys nothing on the headline,
costs a vintage of coverage (a mid-quarter week becomes "no-news"), is not robust to
the variable advance day, and is expensive. **Thursday rejected.**

### 2. The deeper finding: masking-only-GDP leaks BEA co-releases in ~88% of quarters

Matteo's question: if other data is published on the release-week Friday, the masked
nowcast uses it while a true pre-advance forecast would not — is that a bias?

`scan` phase answers it across all quarters. For each quarter Q: `advance` = first
ALFRED `realtime_start` of Q's GDP; `release-Fri` = first manifest vintage ≥ advance;
count non-GDP series with a release in `[advance, release-Fri]` (what the masked
release vintage holds beyond an as-of-`(advance−1)` forecast).

```
quarters scanned: 33
advance-day leak  (intrinsic to masking-only-GDP): 17/33 quarters (max 6 series)
full-window leak  (what Friday+mask actually suffers): 29/33 quarters (max 11 series)
clean quarters (0): 2017Q1, 2018Q1, 2018Q3, 2019Q1   <- all early, all GDP-only advances
```

The leaking series are exactly the BEA national-accounts co-releases — `PCEC96`
(real PCE, ≈68% of GDP), `DSPIC96` (real disposable income), `PCEPI`/`PCEPILFE`
(PCE prices), `A261RX1Q020SBEA` (quarterly BEA, released *with* the advance) — plus
Census durable goods (`DGORDER`, `AMDMTI/UO/VS`) and occasionally housing
(`HSN1F`, `PERMIT`) and jobs (`PAYEMS`, `UNRATE`, `TTLCONS`).

**Conclusion:** masking only the GDP cell is **not** a clean pre-advance forecast.
2017Q1 (on which gate item 1 was validated) is one of just **4 clean quarters out of
33** — exactly the type where the leak is invisible. The earlier "masking recovers a
genuine release-week forecast" claim holds *narrowly* for the masked cell but does not
give a leak-free headline in 29/33 quarters.

### Two legitimate but different quantities

- **Concurrent-cutoff nowcast (NY-Fed convention = current masking):** all release-day
  data except the target. Includes co-releases *by convention*.
- **Genuine pre-advance forecast (chosen):** cut off *before* the BEA release →
  no target, no co-releases.

Gate item 4 originally said "NY-Fed style", which is the concurrent convention; the
decision tightens it to a true pre-advance forecast for the headline.

---

## The decision and why `(advance − 1)`

For each quarter, the headline final nowcast is computed from the vintage **as-of
`advance_date(Q) − 1 day`**. By construction this:

- excludes the GDP target (not released yet) → **no masking needed**, and the
  `News_DFM` FORECAST branch / shape-bug is never reached;
- excludes the same-day BEA co-releases (real PCE, income, PCE prices, …);
- excludes all post-advance / pre-Friday data;
- is **robust to the variable advance day** (defined from each quarter's actual
  advance, not a fixed weekday);
- is reconstructable from ALFRED (daily granularity → `advance − 1` is a clean as-of).

It is the per-quarter, advance-anchored generalization of the "Thursday" idea, done
right. A coarser leak-free alternative (last Friday strictly before the advance) was
considered; `(advance − 1)` was chosen as the tightest pre-advance cutoff.

## Consequences

- **Masking (`mask_target`, shipped in `f50a353`/`e8b8258`):** demoted from headline
  mechanism to an optional *concurrent-cutoff diagnostic*. Default `False`, harmless;
  **not removed** (the v1-parity goldens stay byte-identical).
- **Runner (`nowcast/runner.py`):** the weekly Friday loop drives the within-quarter
  nowcast evolution (mid-quarter, pre-advance, uncontaminated). Phase-4 body must add
  a final **off-grid headline step**: build the `(advance − 1)` vintage per quarter and
  compute its nowcast as the figure scored against the advance.
- **Effect size (pp):** the scan proves the leak *exists* (counts); how many pp it
  moves the headline nowcast is **not yet measured** — it falls out of the Phase-4
  multi-quarter backtest (it will show only in contaminated quarters; 2017Q1 won't).
- **Guardrail:** never validate release-day handling on 2017Q1 alone — it is 1 of 4
  clean quarters. Always check a contaminated quarter (e.g. 2025Q1: leaks real PCE).
