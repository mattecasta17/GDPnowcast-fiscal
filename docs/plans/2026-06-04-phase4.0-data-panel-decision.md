# Phase 4.0 gate — data-panel decision (2026-06-04)

**Status:** DECIDED. Resolves the **last** open gate item, **3** (data panel
truncate-vs-fuller). With items 1/2/4 settled in
`2026-06-04-phase4.0-release-day-cutoff-decision.md`, the **Phase 4.0
evaluation-methodology gate is now CLOSED**.

**Decision:** the headline backtest runs on the **fuller, corrected v2 ALFRED
panel** (`data/US_new` for baseline, `data/US_fiscal` for fiscal). `sample_start`
stays **2000-01-01** (matches v1 production params). The v1 backup panel
(`data/US_new_v1` / `data/US_fiscal_v1`) is kept **only** as the locked
golden-parity fixture, not as a headline input.

---

## Why this is a smaller decision than it looks

The DFM estimation window is bounded by `_SAMPLE_START = pd.Timestamp("2000-01-01")`
(`src/gdpnowcast/nowcast/runner.py:31`), applied in `transform.py:72-73`
(`keep = time >= sample_start`). So **anything before 2000 is dropped regardless of
how far back the panel reaches.** The v2-vs-v1 panel difference was measured in
Phase 2 (`tools/compare_to_v1.py`, writeup in `docs/data_sources.md`); under the
2000 window it collapses to exactly **two** in-window differences, **both strictly
in v2's favour**:

1. **`PCEC96` (real PCE, ≈68% of GDP):** v1's panel starts **2002-01**, v2's starts
   1985-01. Under `sample_start=2000` the pre-2000 extra is moot, but the **24
   months 2000-01 … 2001-12 sit *inside* the estimation window** — v2 has real
   point-in-time values there, v1 leaves them NaN. Genuine, valid, not look-ahead.

2. **Quarterly mis-stamping in ~10 in-window year-end vintages** (`2017-12-29`,
   `2018-12-28`, … `2025-01-03`, plus `2017-02-03`): v1 places
   `GDPC1`/`ULCNFB`/`A261RX1Q020SBEA` on **May/Aug/Nov** instead of the quarter's
   last month **Mar/Jun/Sep** (a spurious +2-month shift — Nov isn't even a
   quarter-end). v2 is the consistent one — it matches v1's *own* stamping in the
   other 476 vintages.

Everything else is a wash: **where both panels populate a cell the values are
identical** (max abs delta = 0; v1's baseline values were themselves ALFRED
point-in-time), and `DGORDER`/`BUSINV`'s extra history (1985 vs 1992) is entirely
pre-2000 → dropped by the window → zero effect.

## Why fuller, not truncate

The classic argument for truncating to v1's series starts is "isolate the
methodology change from the data change so any delta vs v1 `Results.pdf` is
attributable to method alone." Under `sample_start=2000` that argument is weak and
points the wrong way:

- The only two data differences are a **gap-fill of valid PCEC96 history** and a
  **bug-fix of v1's stamping**. Truncating to the v1 panel would mean *deliberately
  re-introducing v1's quarterly-stamping bug into the headline* and *throwing away
  two years of valid PCE data on the single largest GDP component* — to make the
  numbers match a panel we already know is wrong.
- That directly contradicts the honest-research mandate: report what the
  **corrected** pipeline produces, do not engineer a match to the v1 story
  (see `feedback-honest-research`).
- Port-correctness attribution does **not** need the headline to run on the v1
  panel — it is already guaranteed by the golden-parity tests, which are pinned to
  `data/US_new_v1` and stay locked (`tests/test_runner_golden.py`,
  `tests/test_news_golden.py`, etc.). That isolation is untouched by this decision.

## Consequences

- **Headline / Phase-4 body:** `run_quarter(..., data_subdir="US_new", ...)` for the
  baseline backtest and `"US_fiscal"` for the fiscal variant. No code change to the
  runner is required for this item — it is variant-/panel-agnostic via `data_subdir`.
- **Golden parity:** unchanged — those tests keep running on `US_new_v1` to prove
  the port is a faithful v1 reproduction. Two panels, two purposes: v1 panel proves
  the *port*, v2 panel produces the *research result*.
- **Optional data-vs-method ablation (appendix, not gated):** re-running the same
  backtest with `data_subdir="US_new_v1"` quantifies how much of the headline shift
  is data vs methodology. It is essentially free (the v1 panel already exists) and
  belongs in an appendix/footnote, not in the gate.
- **Reversibility:** this is a one-argument change (`data_subdir`); trivially
  revisited if a later finding argues for truncation.
