# Headline backtest results

Headline metric (Phase 4.0 gate decision): for each quarter, the DFM nowcast at the
as-of **(advance_date - 1 day)** pre-advance cutoff -- the tightest as-of that provably
excludes the BEA GDP advance and its same-day co-releases -- scored against the BEA
advance estimate. `error = advance - nowcast`; `Bias > 0` means the nowcast under-predicts.
2020 (q1-q4) is a known outlier regime (Phase 7); **ex-2020 is the headline sample**.

_Generated by `tools/run_backtest.py`; data in `docs/dashboard_data/<variant>.json`._

## Notes

- **2025q1 weekly path**: the v1 source (`nowcast_2025.py`) omitted four Fridays (2025-03-28 ... 2025-04-18) from the 2025q1 vintage list; the registry preserves it faithfully. The on-disk vintages exist, so this only thins the 2025q1 weekly chart -- the headline (advance-1) nowcast is a standalone, fully-populated vintage and is unaffected. (Calendar completion is a dashboard-step follow-up.)
- The large ex-2020 misses (2022q1/q2, 2025q1) are genuine real-economy surprises (inventory/net-export-driven contractions the monthly-indicator DFM does not see), not code/data artifacts -- confirmed by adversarial review.
